# Leadership profile

This paragraph is the primary tuning knob for the whole system. It is passed to
the scorer verbatim and describes the reader: what leadership at Selvaag Eiendom
cares about, and — just as importantly — what they don't.

Edit this file rather than the code. One change at a time, so the effect on next
week's digest is attributable. Every edit gets a commit message naming the miss
that prompted it.

---

## Who is reading

Leadership at Selvaag Eiendom — a Norwegian residential property developer. They
develop and sell homes, mostly in and around Oslo, Bærum and Fornebu, plus
selected projects in other Norwegian cities. They are a developer, not a broker
and not a contractor.

## What matters to them

Anything that changes what they can build, where, at what cost, or how well it
will sell:

- Their own companies and projects being written about, positively or negatively.
- Competitors' moves: land acquisitions, project launches, sales figures,
  financial distress. Who is buying what, and at what price.
- Planning and permitting decisions in the municipalities they operate in —
  especially anything that unlocks or blocks residential volume.
- The housing market's direction: prices, absorption, unsold inventory, and the
  interest-rate and lending-rule decisions that drive them.
- Build cost and contractor capacity, including insolvencies among firms they
  might hire.
- Housing policy that changes the rules of the game.

## What does not matter to them

Be strict here. Most of what a naive keyword filter surfaces belongs in this list:

- Individual home sales, celebrity property purchases, and "dream home" features.
- Residential mortgage advice aimed at consumers.
- Interior design, renovation and home-improvement content.
- Rental disputes between private landlords and tenants.
- Commercial, retail, hotel and logistics property, unless a residential
  developer is a party to it or it is on land they might want.
- Construction news about roads, rail, tunnels and infrastructure with no
  housing component.
- National macroeconomic news with no housing transmission.
- Property news from outside Norway, unless it directly involves a Norwegian
  developer.

## How to weigh it

A story naming Selvaag is nearly always worth surfacing. Beyond that, prefer
concrete and local over abstract and national: a specific reguleringsplan
decision in Bærum beats a general piece on Norwegian housing sentiment. Prefer
news over commentary — a signed opinion column is rarely worth a leader's time
unless it is by someone whose view moves policy.

Under fifteen stories a day. A digest nobody finishes reading has failed.
